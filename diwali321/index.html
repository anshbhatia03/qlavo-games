Engine rules

Group 1: exactly one card.

Group 2: exactly two cards.

Group 3: exactly three cards.

Group total: add every card. A=14, K=13, Q=12, J=11.

Money: choose a 🪙 value per point before the first deal.

Set accounting: the table benchmark is 3 ÷ players (1.5 heads-up, 1 with three, 0.75 with four). Your net = group shares − benchmark. Always zero-sum.

Tie: the group share is divided equally between tied players.
