Quick rules

Turn: draw 2, then play up to 3 cards. If your hand is empty at turn start, draw 5.

Win: first player with 3 complete sets in different colors and at least $10M in cash.

Rent: choose the color on a Rent card. Two-color Rent charges all opponents; Wild Rent targets one.

Debt: pay from your cash pile, properties, or both. Property cards paid for rent/debt stay property cards and never enter the cash pile. No change.

Buildings: one House then one Hotel on each complete set, never Railroads or Utilities.

Wilds: choose or move their displayed color during your turn.
